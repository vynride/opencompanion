opencompanion turns a spare Android phone into a small desk companion. It
listens for a wake word, answers with a voice, and shows an expressive face.

It talks to any OpenAI-compatible API for speech recognition, the language
model and the voice. Wake-word detection and voice-activity detection run
entirely on the phone.

The companion can look through the camera, remember facts across days, search
the web, check the weather, set timers, and mirror notes to your laptop
through KDE Connect.

A wake-word model is not bundled; train your own with openWakeWord and import
it in the settings.
